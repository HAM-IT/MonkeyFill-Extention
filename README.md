**Stop sending the same generic CV to every job. Start tailoring in seconds.**

---

## The Problem

You find a job posting that looks perfect. You know your CV needs tweaking — different skills highlighted, bullets reframed, summary rewritten. But doing this manually for every application takes 30–60 minutes. So you don't. You send the same generic CV and hope for the best.

**Result?** Your application lands in the reject pile before a human ever sees it. ATS systems and recruiters scan for relevance — and a one-size-fits-all CV doesn't cut it.

## The Solution

MonkeyFill reads the job posting, understands what the employer actually wants, and rebuilds your CV around it — in under 30 seconds.

- **No copy-pasting into ChatGPT.** No prompt engineering. No reformatting.
- **Your real experience**, reframed to match what this specific job values.
- **One click** from job posting to tailored, print-ready PDF.

Upload your master CV once. MonkeyFill does the rest — every time, for every job.

---

## ✨ What It Does

### 🧠 Job Match Analysis
Paste a job description or point MonkeyFill at the page. It will:
- **Score your match** (0–100%) using a weighted ATS rubric
- **Show matched vs. missing skills** so you know where you stand
- **Give honest coaching advice** — what to fix, how to bridge gaps
- **Build a growth roadmap** — skills to learn, projects to build, certifications worth getting
- **Suggest a stand-out move** — one creative, specific action to differentiate you

### 📄 Tailored CV Generation
From the same job posting, MonkeyFill generates a job-specific CV:
- Skills reordered by relevance to this role
- Experience bullets reframed around what the employer cares about
- A professional summary written specifically for this job
- Download as a clean, print-ready PDF — ready to submit

### 🎨 Themes & Layouts
5 color themes (Classic, Modern Trust, Sustainable, Sophisticated, Energetic) and 3 layout templates (Modern Sidebar, Classic Executive, Bold Split).

### 🌍 Multi-Language
Generate tailored CVs in English or French.

## 🚀 Installation

1. Download the latest `.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/HAM-IT/MonkeyFill-Extention/releases/latest)
2. Unzip the folder
3. Open Chrome → go to `chrome://extensions`
4. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in top-right)
5. Click **"Load unpacked"** → select the unzipped MonkeyFill folder
6. Pin the 🐒 icon to your toolbar — you're ready

---

## 📄 Getting Started

1. Click the MonkeyFill icon → **Settings ⚙️**
2. Upload your **master CV** (PDF)
3. MonkeyFill extracts your professional profile locally in your browser
4. Find a job posting → click MonkeyFill → paste the description → go

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

- Your CV is extracted **locally in your browser** using PDF.js — the raw file never leaves your machine
- AI analysis runs through a secure backend proxy — your API keys are never exposed
- When you upload a CV, a summary of your professional profile (name, contact info, current position, top skills) is stored on our servers to enable service features
- We **do not sell or share** your personal data with third parties
- No tracking. No ads. No third-party analytics.
